Wesleyan Methodist Church

You have arrived at Aberdeen's Wesleyan Methodist Church, an important part of the town's religious heritage. The building dates from the early eighteen eighties, when different Christian congregations were establishing their own places of worship. Its more modest scale provides a contrast with the commanding Dutch Reformed Church. Notice the stonework, pointed openings and the character of its tower. The story here is not only about architecture, but about the families, ministers and congregants who gathered for worship and helped shape everyday community life. Churches also became places where friendships formed, people supported one another, and the town marked important moments. As you continue, look for the variety of faith traditions represented in Aberdeen's historic streets.
