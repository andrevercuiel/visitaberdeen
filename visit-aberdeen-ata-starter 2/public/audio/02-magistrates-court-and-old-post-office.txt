Magistrates Court and Old Post Office

Welcome to Aberdeen's historic magistrate's court and old post office. This impressive complex dates to 1898 and tells the story of a Karoo town growing into an organised community. Look closely at its roofline and decorative details. Architectural historians note an unusual mixture of Scottish Baronial, Flemish Revival and Art Nouveau influences. The whimsical terracotta creatures high on the building are especially memorable. In earlier years, the court and postal services connected residents with administration, communication and the wider world, often across great distances. Picture letters arriving by post, disputes being heard, and neighbours exchanging news outside. Today the building reminds us that civic services were once housed in structures intended to impress. Please admire the exterior respectfully, without entering restricted areas.
