All Saints Anglican Church

Our fourth stop introduces Aberdeen's Anglican heritage. All Saints stands as another reminder that the town grew through the contributions of several congregations, not just one. The historic Anglican church is associated with the late nineteenth century, an era when Gothic Revival features shaped many church buildings in South Africa. Look for pointed arches and the quiet proportions that give these buildings their distinctive atmosphere. Think of the baptisms, weddings, memorial services and weekly gatherings that connected generations of local families. Aberdeen's heritage is made up of many communities and many memories, including stories not always recorded in official histories. Take a moment to appreciate the building and its setting before moving on.
