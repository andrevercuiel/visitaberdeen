Pagel House

Welcome to Pagel House, a Victorian home with a surprising story. Built in 1897 during a prosperous period for ostrich feathers and wool, it was originally called Claremont House. An early owner was ostrich-feather trader R. C. Logie Junior. Later, the property became associated with Frank Wilke, who owned a private zoo near Aberdeen and admired the famous circus strongman Wilhelm Pagel. Wilke acquired name plaques connected with the Pagel Circus, helping give the house the name it carries today. The building has since been restored and used as a guest house. This stop links the grand houses of the Karoo with commerce, entertainment, travel and an unexpected connection to circus history. Please remember it is a working accommodation property and respect guests' privacy.
