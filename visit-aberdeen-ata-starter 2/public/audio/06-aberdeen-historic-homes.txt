Aberdeen Historic Homes

As you explore Aberdeen's historic residential streets, slow down and look carefully at the buildings around you. Victorian houses stand alongside simpler Karoo homes, reflecting changing tastes, fortunes and building traditions. Verandas, sash windows, decorative trim and sturdy walls each tell part of the story. During the nineteenth century, farming, wool and ostrich feathers shaped the local economy, and some residents expressed their prosperity through elaborate homes. Yet the town's history was also made by builders, labourers, domestic workers, traders and families whose names may not appear on plaques. These streets are lived-in neighbourhoods, not an open-air museum. Enjoy the architectural detail from public spaces, and please do not enter private properties without permission.
