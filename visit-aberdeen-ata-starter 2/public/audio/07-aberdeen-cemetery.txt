Aberdeen Cemetery

The Aberdeen cemetery is a place for quiet reflection. Gravestones here preserve names, languages, dates and family connections that tell us about the community across generations. The South African War, also known as the Second Anglo-Boer War, divided people in and around Aberdeen. Historical accounts record that local Cape residents joined Boer forces, creating deep tensions in a district governed by the British Cape Colony. Graves and memorials offer one window into that difficult time, although they never tell every side of the story. As you walk, remember that each grave represents a person whose life meant something to others. Please remain on permitted paths, protect the stones, and be considerate of people visiting loved ones.
